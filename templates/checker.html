Emergency Red Flags

If any of the following occur, do not use this app. Seek emergency hospital care immediately:

  • Persistent crushing chest pressure or pain
  • Sudden confusion or inability to speak
  • Bluish lips, face, or nailbeds
  • Temperature above 104°F (40°C)
