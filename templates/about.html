Mathematical Formulation & Metrics

Theoretical framework governing model evaluation and training.

1. Accuracy Score

The fraction of predictions the model got right across all 16 target disease classes:

Accuracy = (TP + TN) / (TP + TN + FP + FN)

2. Precision & Recall

Precision assesses clinical relevance, while Recall measures sensitivity against false negatives:

Precision = TP / (TP + FP) | Recall = TP / (TP + FN)

3. Weighted F1-Score

Harmonic mean balancing precision and recall across all condition categories:

F1 = 2 × (Precision × Recall) / (Precision + Recall)

4. Gini Impurity (Tree Splits)

Measures node purity across decision trees to determine the best symptom split:

Gini = 1 - \sum_{i=1}^{C} (p_i)^2

Responsible AI & Ethical Healthcare Principles

Guiding design decisions for patient safety and transparency.

1. Transparent Disclaimers: Every screen clearly discloses that MediPredict AI is an educational computer science demo, preventing diagnostic reliance.
2. Rule-Based Triage Safety Net: Red-flag indicators immediately bypass model nuances to display urgent emergency warnings with emergency hotline numbers.
3. Privacy by Design: No names, phone numbers, IP addresses, or cookies are logged. All historical records are saved strictly on the local machine.
4. Multi-Condition Differentials: Replaces naive "single disease certainty" with ranked differential probabilities, acknowledging the reality of symptom ambiguity.
