Internship & Viva Defense Guide

Key technical concepts, theoretical trade-offs, and expected presentation questions.

Q1: Why use Stratified K-Fold over standard K-Fold?

In healthcare multi-class classification, symptom datasets must preserve the percentage of samples for each disease class across every fold. Stratified K-Fold guarantees identical class distributions in both training and validation subsets, preventing under-representation of rare pathologies.

Q2: Why does Random Forest outperform a single Decision Tree?

A single Decision Tree is prone to high variance and overfitting noisy symptom combinations. Random Forest uses bootstrap aggregation (bagging) with random feature subspace sampling, creating an ensemble of decorrelated trees whose averaged vote drastically reduces variance without increasing bias.

Q3: How are differential diagnosis probabilities calculated?

In Scikit-learn, probabilities are computed via predict_proba. For Logistic Regression, the softmax function converts raw log-odds into a normalized probability distribution across all 16 classes (\(\sum p_i = 1\)). For Random Forest, it computes the fraction of voting trees supporting each class.

Q4: What are the primary ethical and safety constraints?

Medical AI systems cannot assess patient history, vital signs (SpO2, blood pressure), or lab tests. MediPredict AI implements defensive engineering: automated red-flag triage, differential probabilities instead of single labels, and prominent safety disclaimers to prevent diagnostic misuse.
